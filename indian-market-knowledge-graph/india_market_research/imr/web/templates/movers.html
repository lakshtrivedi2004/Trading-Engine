How this is calculated
  • Move: close vs BSE's previous close (BSE adjusts it for splits and bonuses, so they don't show as fake crashes).
  • vs own normal (σ): today's move divided by the stock's daily volatility over the previous 20 sessions (today excluded). ±3σ is rare for that stock.
  • Volatility: standard deviation of the last 20 daily moves, annualised. ATR: average true range over 14 days, % of price (Wilder 1978).
  • Expected next move: pooled HAR volatility forecast (Corsi 2009) — the typical size of the next session's move, not its direction. Its accuracy on unseen days is shown in the card above.
  • Flags: ⛔ circuit = locked or closing right at a 2/5/10/20% band, may not be tradable at that price; T/XT = trade-to-trade (no intraday); Z = compliance issues; M = SME.
  • Excluded from the lists: {% for k, v in scan.excluded.items() %}{{ k }} ({{ v }}){% if not loop.last %}, {% endif %}{% endfor %}. Change the filters in config/settings.toml → [scanner].
