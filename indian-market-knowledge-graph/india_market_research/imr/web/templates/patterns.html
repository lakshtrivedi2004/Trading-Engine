Research-backed rules are tested on older data (in-sample) and then checked on newer data they have never seen (out-of-sample). Signals use only information available at day t's close and are applied to day t+1; every rule passes an automatic look-ahead check first. Costs are deducted.

1 bp = 0.01%. Include brokerage, STT, exchange charges and slippage. Every run is recorded: changing a setting and re-running is another test.
