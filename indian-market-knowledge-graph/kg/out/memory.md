## M01 Introduction to Stock Markets
- `investing` [Concept] Investing: Deploying surplus cash into asset classes to fight inflation, create wealth and meet financial goals. | → RELATED_TO:inflation, USES:asset_allocation, USES:compounding
- `asset_class` [Concept] Asset Class: A category of investment with particular risk and return characteristics - fixed income, equity, real estate, commodities (precious metals). | → RELATED_TO:asset_allocation, RELATED_TO:equity, RELATED_TO:fixed_income

- `equity` [Instrument] Equity (Shares): Part-ownership of a publicly listed company traded on BSE/NSE; highest long-run return, no capital guarantee. | → IS_A:asset_class, RELATED_TO:stock_exchange, RELATED_TO:corporate_actions
- `asset_allocation` [Concept] Asset Allocation: Allocating money across asset classes based on risk appetite. | → USES:asset_class, RELATED_TO:diversification
- `stock_exchange` [Participant] Stock Exchange (NSE / BSE): Electronic marketplace matching buyers and sellers; India's two main exchanges are NSE and BSE. | → REGULATED_BY:sebi, RELATED_TO:stock_broker, RELATED_TO:clearing_corporation
- `market_participants` [Participant] Market Participants: Domestic retail, NRI/OCI, domestic institutions (e.g. LIC), domestic AMCs (mutual funds), Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs). | → REGULATED_BY:sebi, RELATED_TO:fii
- `fii` [Participant] Foreign Institutional Investors (FII/FPI): Foreign corporates/funds/individuals investing in India in large size; flows impact sentiment. | Bot: Track FII net buy/sell flows as a sentiment input. | → IS_A:market_participants
- `sebi` [Participant] SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India): India's securities market regulator - develops exchanges, protects retail investors, regulates participants and intermediaries. | → RELATED_TO:ipo
- `stock_broker` [Participant] Stock Broker: SEBI-registered trading member of an exchange; your gateway to markets via a trading account. | → REGULATED_BY:sebi, RELATED_TO:trading_account, RELATED_TO:contract_note
- `trading_account` [Concept] Trading Account: Account with a broker used to buy/sell securities; linked to a DEMAT account and exactly one bank account. | → RELATED_TO:demat_account
- `demat_account` [Concept] DEMAT Account: Electronic vault holding dematerialised shares, maintained by a depository (NSDL or CDSL) through a Depository Participant. | → PART_OF:depository
- `depository` [Participant] Depository (NSDL / CDSL) and Depository Participant: NSDL and CDSL hold securities electronically; DPs (banks/brokers) are their agents. | → REGULATED_BY:sebi
- `clearing_corporation` [Participant] Clearing Corporation (NSCCL / ICCL): Exchange subsidiaries that guarantee settlement - match debits/credits and prevent default. | → RELATED_TO:clearing_and_settlement
- `company_funding_lifecycle` [Process] Company Funding Lifecycle: Seed (promoter + angels) -> Series A (VC) -> Series B -> Series C (Private Equity) -> IPO; valuation rises as revenue/profit validate business. | F: implied_valuation=investment_amount / stake_fraction | → PREREQUISITE:ipo, RELATED_TO:capex, RELATED_TO:face_value
- `capex` [Concept] Capital Expenditure (Capex): Spending to expand/improve the business (plants, stores, capacity).
- `face_value` [Concept] Face Value (Par Value): Fixed nominal denomination of a share; dividends and splits are referenced to it. | → RELATED_TO:dividend, RELATED_TO:stock_split
- `ipo` [Process] Initial Public Offering (IPO): First offer of a company's shares to the public, moving it from private to listed (primary -> secondary market). | Bot: Subscription multiple and anchor/VC pedigree are pre-listing sentiment features; listing day = start of secondary trading. | ⚠ Investors should read the DRHP; some companies list without healthy prior funding rounds. | → REGULATED_BY:sebi, USES:merchant_banker, USES:drhp, PART_OF:primary_market, RELATED_TO:fpo, RELATED_TO:ofs
- `merchant_banker` [Participant] Merchant Banker (BRLM / Lead Manager): Assists IPO - due diligence, DRHP, underwriting, price band, road shows, appointing registrars/bankers. | → RELATED_TO:ipo, REGULATED_BY:sebi
- `drhp` [Concept] Draft Red Herring Prospectus (DRHP): Public IPO disclosure document with business, financials, use of proceeds, risks and management. | → PART_OF:ipo
- `primary_market` [Concept] Primary vs Secondary Market: Primary market = IPO bidding/issuance; secondary market = trading on exchange after listing. | → RELATED_TO:stock_exchange
- `fpo` [Process] Follow-on Public Offer (FPO): Post-listing public issue of fresh (dilutive) shares via DRHP + book building, used to fund new projects; rare since OFS (2012). | → CONTRADICTS:ofs
- `ofs` [Process] Offer for Sale (OFS): Exchange window for promoters to sell existing shares (no new shares) at/above a floor price; also to meet minimum public shareholding. | → RELATED_TO:fpo
- `price_drivers` [Concept] What Moves Stock Prices: Prices move on news/events (company, industry, economy) and expectations, and on demand-supply imbalance even without news. | Bot: News/event sentiment and sector contagion features; liquidity filter - illiquid names don't respond. | → RELATED_TO:market_events, RELATED_TO:bid_ask
- `holding_period` [Concept] Holding Period: Period you intend to hold a position - minutes to forever. | → RELATED_TO:trader_types
- `absolute_return` [Formula] Absolute Return: Percentage gain irrespective of time. | F: absolute_return_pct=(end_value / start_value - 1) * 100 | C: use_when: holding period <= 1 year | → RELATED_TO:cagr
- `cagr` [Formula] Compounded Annual Growth Rate (CAGR): Annualised growth rate over multiple years. | F: cagr=(end_value / start_value) ** (1 / years) - 1 | C: use_when: holding period > 1 year | → RELATED_TO:compounding
- `trader_types` [Concept] Trader and Investor Types: Traders (short view, unbiased long/short) vs investors (multi-year holding).
- `market_index` [Concept] Stock Market Index (Nifty / Sensex): Basket of large, liquid stocks acting as a barometer of the market/economy; Nifty (NSE) and Sensex (BSE). | Bot: Benchmark strategy returns against Nifty; use index as regime/market-trend filter; hedge with index futures. | → USES:free_float_market_cap, RELATED_TO:index_futures, RELATED_TO:hedging, RELATED_TO:sectoral_index

- `free_float_market_cap` [Formula] Free Float Market Capitalisation: Market value of shares available for trading; basis of Indian index weights. | F: free_float_mcap=free_float_shares * price; weight_i=ffmcap_i / sum(ffmcap) | → PART_OF:market_index, VARIANT_OF:market_capitalisation
- `bull_bear_market` [Concept] Bull / Bear Market: Bullish = expecting rise (index rising period = bull market); bearish = expecting fall. | → RELATED_TO:trend
- `trend` [Concept] Trend: General market direction and its strength - bullish, bearish or sideways. | → RELATED_TO:dow_theory
- `week52_high_low` [Concept] 52-Week High / Low and All-Time High / Low: Highest/lowest traded price over last 52 weeks (or since listing). | C: bullish_hint: close >= max(high, 252 sessions); bearish_hint: close <= min(low, 252 sessions) | Bot: Breakout/momentum filter. | → RELATED_TO:breakout
- `long_position` [Concept] Long Position: Buy first, sell later; bullish; profit if price rises. | → OPPOSITE_OF:short_position, RELATED_TO:square_off
- `short_position` [Concept] Short Position (Shorting): Sell first (borrowed), buy back later; bearish; profit if price falls, loss if it rises. | → RELATED_TO:futures_contract
- `square_off` [Concept] Square Off: Closing an existing position - sell to close a long, buy to close a short (not a new short/long). | → RELATED_TO:intraday_position
- `intraday_position` [Concept] Intraday Position: Position initiated with intent to square off the same day. | → RELATED_TO:mis_product
- `ohlc` [Concept] OHLC and Volume: Open, high, low, close prices of a period; volume = total shares transacted. | → RELATED_TO:candlestick, RELATED_TO:volume
- `market_segment` [Concept] Market Segments: Capital market (equity 'EQ', preference shares, warrants, ETFs), Futures & Options (leveraged derivatives), Wholesale Debt Market (G-secs, T-bills, bonds). | → RELATED_TO:derivatives
- `trading_terminal` [Product] Trading Terminal / Market Watch: Broker software to view quotes (LTP, % change, OHLC, volume), market depth and place/modify orders. | → RELATED_TO:order_types, RELATED_TO:bid_ask
- `order_types` [Concept] Order Types (Limit, Market, SL, SL-M): Limit = specific price (may not fill); Market = immediate at available prices (price uncertainty); SL/SL-M = passive stop that activates at trigger price. | C: long_stop: {"trigger_price": "> stop_price", "activates_when": "ltp <= trigger_price"} | Bot: Prefer limit orders to control slippage; place SL/SL-M orders immediately after entry. | → RELATED_TO:stop_loss, RELATED_TO:bid_ask, RELATED_TO:product_types
- `product_types` [Concept] Product Types (CNC, MIS, NRML): CNC = delivery (hold in demat); MIS = intraday margin product (auto square-off); NRML = normal (carry-forward F&O). | → RELATED_TO:mis_product, RELATED_TO:margin
- `mis_product` [Product] MIS (Margin Intraday Square-off): Intraday leveraged product squared off same day. | → PART_OF:product_types
- `bid_ask` [Concept] Bid, Ask and Market Depth: Bid = best prices buyers will pay; ask/offer = prices sellers demand; depth shows top 5 levels each side. | F: spread=best_ask - best_bid; mid=(best_ask + best_bid)/2 | Bot: Estimate slippage by walking the depth for order size; use bid/ask totals as order-flow imbalance feature. | → MEASURES:liquidity, RELATED_TO:impact_cost
- `clearing_and_settlement` [Process] Clearing and Settlement (T+2 per source): Buy on T day - funds + charges debited T; shares credited to demat end of T+2 (per source); sale proceeds credited T+2. | ⚠ Source describes T+2; India moved to T+1 settlement in 2023 - verify current cycle. | → USES:clearing_corporation, RELATED_TO:btst, RELATED_TO:contract_note
- `btst` [Strategy] BTST / ATST: Selling shares bought the previous day before they reach demat. | ⚠ Short-delivery/auction risk especially in illiquid and B-group stocks; avoid unless risk understood.
- `contract_note` [Concept] Contract Note: Legal record of trades and charges issued by broker on trade day. | → RELATED_TO:transaction_charges
- `transaction_charges` [Concept] Transaction Charges (brokerage, STT, exchange fees, GST, SEBI fees, stamp duty): Costs on every trade - brokerage, Securities Transaction Tax, exchange transaction charges, service tax/GST, SEBI turnover fee. | Bot: Always deduct full round-trip costs in backtests; costs dominate high-frequency strategies. | ⚠ Rates in source are dated; use current schedule. | → RELATED_TO:stt
- `corporate_actions` [Event] Corporate Actions: Board-initiated, shareholder-approved actions that change the stock - dividends, bonus, split, rights, buyback. | Bot: Adjust historical prices for splits/bonuses; flag ex-dates to avoid false gaps in signals. | → RELATED_TO:dividend, RELATED_TO:bonus_issue, RELATED_TO:stock_split, RELATED_TO:rights_issue, RELATED_TO:buyback
- `dividend` [Event] Dividend: Distribution of profits (or reserves) per share, expressed as % of face value; interim or final. | Bot: Price drops roughly by dividend amount on ex-date - adjust backtests; dividend arbitrage awareness. | → PART_OF:corporate_actions, USES:face_value, RELATED_TO:dividend_yield
- `bonus_issue` [Event] Bonus Issue: Free shares from reserves in a fixed ratio (e.g. 2:1 = 2 new per 1 held); share count rises, price adjusts, value unchanged. | F: post_price=pre_price * held / (held + bonus) | → PART_OF:corporate_actions, RELATED_TO:stock_split
- `stock_split` [Event] Stock Split: Face value split (e.g. FV 10 -> 5); shares multiply, price divides, market cap unchanged. | F: split_factor=old_fv / new_fv; post_price=pre_price / split_factor | → PART_OF:corporate_actions, USES:face_value
- `rights_issue` [Event] Rights Issue: Fresh capital raised from existing shareholders in proportion to holding (e.g. 1:4) at a discount; dilutive. | ⚠ Don't be swayed by discount; if market price < rights price buy in market instead. | → PART_OF:corporate_actions
- `buyback` [Event] Share Buyback: Company buys back its own shares, reducing share count. | → PART_OF:corporate_actions, AFFECTS:eps
- `market_events` [Event] Key Macro Events: Monetary policy, inflation, IIP, PMI, Budget and quarterly earnings move markets. | → RELATED_TO:monetary_policy, RELATED_TO:inflation, RELATED_TO:iip, RELATED_TO:pmi, RELATED_TO:union_budget, RELATED_TO:earnings_announcement
- `monetary_policy` [Event] RBI Monetary Policy (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR): RBI balances growth vs inflation via key rates; reviewed periodically (quarterly per source). | Bot: Event-day volatility regime; sector rotation on rate decisions. | → AFFECTS:inflation, RELATED_TO:interest_rates
- `interest_rates` [Concept] Interest Rates: Cost of money; drives borrowing, growth, inflation, bond prices, currency and futures pricing.
- `inflation` [Concept] Inflation (CPI / WPI): Sustained rise in general prices measured by WPI (wholesale) and CPI (retail); MOSPI publishes CPI ~2nd week monthly. | → RELATED_TO:monetary_policy
- `iip` [Event] Index of Industrial Production (IIP): Monthly industrial production index by MOSPI; rising = positive for markets; low IIP pressures RBI to cut rates. | → PART_OF:market_events
- `pmi` [Event] Purchasing Managers Index (PMI): Survey-based manufacturing/services activity index. | C: expansion: pmi > 50; contraction: pmi < 50; no_change: pmi == 50 | → PART_OF:market_events
- `union_budget` [Event] Union Budget: Annual finance ministry presentation (around Feb 1 now; last week of Feb per source) with policy/tax changes; strong market reactions. | → PART_OF:market_events
- `earnings_announcement` [Event] Quarterly Earnings Announcement: Listed companies report quarterly revenue, expenses, taxes, profit and sometimes guidance. | C: signal: sign(actual - consensus); in_line: flat_to_negative_bias | Bot: Earnings-surprise strategies; avoid holding through results unless intended; widen stops/IV crush awareness. | → PART_OF:market_events, RELATED_TO:iv_crush
- `point_of_view` [Concept] Point of View (POV) and Instrument Selection: Success requires a researched directional view (via FA, TA, quant) plus choosing the right instrument to express it. | Bot: Map signal horizon/conviction to instrument - equity for long-term, futures for short-term directional, options for capped-risk/event trades. | → USES:technical_analysis, USES:fundamental_analysis, USES:quantitative_analysis, RELATED_TO:futures_contract, RELATED_TO:options

## M02 Technical Analysis
- `technical_analysis` [Concept] Technical Analysis (TA): Research technique identifying trading opportunities from market participants' actions (price/volume) visualised as charts and patterns. | → CONTRADICTS:fundamental_analysis, USES:ohlc, USES:candlestick, USES:ta_checklist, RELATED_TO:dow_theory
- `close_price_importance` [Concept] Closing Price is Most Important: Close is the final evaluation of the period, the reference for next session and the basis of most indicators; close > open = positive day. | → PART_OF:ohlc, PART_OF:dow_theory
- `chart_types` [Concept] Chart Types (Line, Bar, Candlestick): Line chart plots closes only (trend view, little info); bar chart shows OHLC with left tick open/right tick close (hard to read); Japanese candlestick is the default. | → RELATED_TO:candlestick
- `candlestick` [Concept] Japanese Candlestick Anatomy: Real body connects open and close; upper shadow to high, lower shadow to low; bullish (blue/green) if close > open, bearish (red) if close < open. | F: body=abs(close - open); upper_shadow=high - max(open, close); lower_shadow=min(open, close) - low; range=high - low; bullish=close > open; bearish=close < open | → USES:ohlc, RELATED_TO:candlestick_rules
- `timeframe` [Concept] Timeframes and Look-back Period: Monthly (12 candles/yr), weekly (52), daily (~252), intraday 30m (~12/day), 15m (25/day), 5m (75/day); choose to filter information from noise. | P: {"daily_candles_per_year": 252, "swing_lookback_months": [6, 12], "sr_lookback_years": 2} | → PART_OF:technical_analysis
- `candlestick_rules` [Rule] Candlestick Trading Rules (3 assumptions): (1) Buy strength, sell weakness - buy on a blue day, sell on a red day; (2) be flexible but quantify and verify deviations; (3) look for a prior trend - bullish patterns need a prior downtrend, bearish need a prior uptrend. | C: confirmation_long: next_day.close > next_day.open; confirmation_short: next_day.close < next_day.open; tradable_range: 0.01 <= (high - low)/close <= 0.10; stop_long: min(low over pattern candles); stop_short: max(high over pattern candles) | Bot: Implement pattern detectors with tolerance params; require prior trend filter; enter at close of signal bar (aggressive) or next bar close with colour confirmation (conservative). | → USES:candlestick, USES:prior_trend, RELATED_TO:support_resistance
- `prior_trend` [Rule] Prior Trend Requirement: Reversal patterns are valid only after an opposing trend; a perfect hammer without a prior downtrend is not a pattern. | C: prior_downtrend_proxy: close[t-1] < close[t-1-n] (e.g. n=5..10) or close below falling short EMA; prior_uptrend_proxy: close[t-1] > close[t-1-n] or close above rising short EMA | ⚠ Source defines prior trend visually; the proxies are implementation choices. | → PART_OF:candlestick_rules, USES:trend
- `bullish_marubozu` [CandlestickPattern] Bullish Marubozu: Long blue candle with no shadows (open = low, close = high) showing buying at every price; bullish regardless of prior trend. | C: identify: close > open and abs(open - low) <= tol*high and abs(high - close) <= tol*high; tol_example: ACC 0.17% deviation accepted; entry: near close of marubozu (risk taker) or next day close if next day is blue (risk averse); stop: low of marubozu; target: nearest resistance | → OPPOSITE_OF:bearish_marubozu, USES:candlestick_rules
- `bearish_marubozu` [CandlestickPattern] Bearish Marubozu: Long red candle with no shadows (open = high, close = low); extreme selling; bearish regardless of prior trend. | C: identify: close < open and abs(high - open) <= tol*high and abs(close - low) <= tol*high; entry: short near close (or next day close if red); stop: high of marubozu
- `spinning_top` [CandlestickPattern] Spinning Top: Small real body with roughly equal upper and lower shadows; colour irrelevant; indecision - bulls and bears both failed. | C: identify: body small relative to range and upper_shadow ~= lower_shadow | Bot: Treat as volatility-expansion/indecision flag; reduce size or scale out; not an entry signal alone. | → RELATED_TO:doji
- `doji` [CandlestickPattern] Doji: Open ~= close (no or wafer-thin body), shadows any length; indecision like spinning top; often appear in clusters. | C: identify: abs(close - open) <= small_frac * (high - low) | → CONFIRMS:bearish_engulfing
- `paper_umbrella` [CandlestickPattern] Paper Umbrella: Small body near top of range with long lower shadow >= 2x body; called Hammer after a downtrend, Hanging Man after an uptrend. | C: identify: lower_shadow >= 2*body and upper_shadow small | → RELATED_TO:hammer, RELATED_TO:hanging_man
- `hammer` [CandlestickPattern] Hammer: Paper umbrella at the bottom of a downtrend; bulls rejected new lows; bullish reversal. Longer lower shadow = more bullish; blue body slightly preferred. | C: prior_trend: downtrend; identify: lower_shadow >= 2*body and abs(close-open)/open within ~1-2% and close near high; entry: near close of hammer day (risk taker) or next day close if blue (risk averse); stop: low of hammer | → VARIANT_OF:paper_umbrella, OPPOSITE_OF:hanging_man
- `hanging_man` [CandlestickPattern] Hanging Man: Paper umbrella at the top of an uptrend; bears entered intraday; bearish reversal (less reliable than hammer per author because price recovered). | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: lower_shadow >= 2*body; entry: short near close (or next day close if red); stop: high of candle | → VARIANT_OF:paper_umbrella
- `shooting_star` [CandlestickPattern] Shooting Star: Inverted umbrella at top of uptrend - small body near low with upper shadow >= 2x body; bears pushed price back from new high; bearish. Red body slightly more reliable. | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: upper_shadow >= 2*body and lower_shadow small and close ~= low; entry: short near close (or next day if red); stop: high of shooting star | → OPPOSITE_OF:hammer
- `bullish_engulfing` [CandlestickPattern] Bullish Engulfing: Two-candle bottom reversal - P1 red, P2 blue whose real body engulfs P1's real body. | C: prior_trend: downtrend; identify: P1.close < P1.open and P2.close > P2.open and P2.open <= P1.close and P2.close > P1.open; entry: close of P2 (preferred - multi-day pattern) or day after if blue; stop: min(P1.low, P2.low) | → OPPOSITE_OF:bearish_engulfing, VARIANT_OF:piercing_pattern
- `bearish_engulfing` [CandlestickPattern] Bearish Engulfing: Two-candle top reversal - P1 blue, P2 red whose body engulfs P1's body. | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: P1.close > P1.open and P2.close < P2.open and P2.open >= P1.close and P2.close < P1.open; entry: short at close of P2; stop: max(P1.high, P2.high) | → VARIANT_OF:dark_cloud_cover
- `piercing_pattern` [CandlestickPattern] Piercing Pattern: Like bullish engulfing but P2's blue body covers 50% to <100% of P1's red body. | C: prior_trend: downtrend; identify: P1 red, P2 blue, 0.5*P1.body <= (P2.close - P1.close) < P1.body; stop: pattern low | → VARIANT_OF:bullish_engulfing, OPPOSITE_OF:dark_cloud_cover
- `dark_cloud_cover` [CandlestickPattern] Dark Cloud Cover: Like bearish engulfing but P2's red body covers 50% to <100% of P1's blue body; trade setup same as bearish engulfing. | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: P1 blue, P2 red, 0.5*P1.body <= (P1.close - P2.close) < P1.body; stop: pattern high | → VARIANT_OF:bearish_engulfing
- `bullish_harami` [CandlestickPattern] Bullish Harami: Bottom reversal - long red P1 then small blue P2 contained within P1's body ('pregnant'); surprise strength unnerves bears. | C: prior_trend: downtrend; identify: P1 long red; P2 blue; P2.open > P1.close; P2.close < P1.open; entry: close of P2 or next day if blue; stop: min(P1.low, P2.low) | → OPPOSITE_OF:bearish_harami
- `bearish_harami` [CandlestickPattern] Bearish Harami: Top reversal - long blue P1 then small red P2 inside P1's body. | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: P1 long blue; P2 red; P2.open < P1.close; P2.close > P1.open; entry: short at close of P2 or next day if red; stop: max(P1.high, P2.high)
- `gaps` [Concept] Gap Up / Gap Down: Gap up = open above prior close (buyer enthusiasm, bullish); gap down = open below prior close (bearish); no trades occur in the gap. | C: gap_up: open > prev_close (strict: open > prev_high); gap_down: open < prev_close | → PART_OF:morning_star, PART_OF:evening_star
- `morning_star` [CandlestickPattern] Morning Star (and Morning Doji Star): Three-candle bottom reversal - long red P1, gap-down doji/spinning top P2, gap-up blue P3 closing above P1's open. | C: prior_trend: downtrend; identify: P1 red (long); P2 gaps down and is doji/spinning top; P3 gaps up, blue, P3.close > P1.open; entry: close of P3 for both risk types; stop: lowest low of P1..P3 | → OPPOSITE_OF:evening_star, USES:doji, USES:gaps
- `evening_star` [CandlestickPattern] Evening Star (and Evening Doji Star): Three-candle top reversal - long blue P1, gap-up doji/spinning top P2, gap-down red P3 closing below P1's open. | C: prior_trend: uptrend; identify: P1 blue; P2 gaps up, doji/spinning top; P3 gaps down, red, P3.close < P1.open; entry: short at close of P3; stop: highest high of P1..P3
- `support_resistance` [Concept] Support and Resistance (S&R): Price zones expected to attract maximum buying (support, below CMP) or selling (resistance, above CMP); used for targets, entries and stop placement. | C: level_valid: count(swing pivots within +/-tol of level) >= 3 and pivots spaced in time; is_support: level < cmp; is_resistance: level > cmp | Bot: Detect swing pivots, cluster by price with tolerance, keep clusters with >=3 well-spaced touches; use nearest opposite level as target and require stop near a level. | → PART_OF:ta_checklist, USES:reward_risk_ratio, RELATED_TO:pivot_points
- `volume` [Concept] Volume and the Volume-Price Trend Table: Shares traded (one buy + one sell of 100 = volume 100); used to confirm trends; high volume implies institutional 'smart money'. | C: high_volume: volume > SMA(volume, 10); low_volume: volume < SMA(volume, 10); price_up_vol_up: bullish; price_up_vol_down: caution - weak hands buying / possible bull trap; price_down_vol_up: bearish; price_down_vol_down: caution - weak hands selling / possible bear trap | Bot: Volume filter for entries - require volume >= 10-day average on the signal bar. | → PART_OF:ta_checklist, CONFIRMS:breakout
- `moving_average` [Indicator] Moving Averages (SMA / EMA): Rolling average of closes; SMA weights equally, EMA weights recent data more and reacts faster (preferred). ## M05 Options Theory for Professional Trading
- `options` [Instrument] Options (overview): Right but not obligation for the buyer (who pays premium) to buy (call) or sell (put) the underlying at a strike on/before expiry; seller/writer has the obligation. ~80% of Indian derivatives volume (source era). | → PART_OF:call_option, PART_OF:put_option, RELATED_TO:option_greeks, IS_A:derivatives

- `put_option` [Instrument] Put Option (long and short): Buyer bearish - right to sell at strike; seller bullish/neutral, earns limited premium with large risk. | F: intrinsic_value_expiry=max(0, strike - spot); long_put_pnl=max(0, strike - spot) - premium_paid; short_put_pnl=premium_received - max(0, strike - spot); breakeven=strike - premium | → PART_OF:options_payoff_summary

- `moneyness` [Concept] Moneyness (ITM / ATM / OTM): ITM has positive intrinsic value; ATM strike is nearest to spot; OTM has zero intrinsic value. - `delta` [Greek] Delta: Change in premium per 1-point change in underlying; calls 0..1, puts -1..0; ATM ~0.5, ITM -> 1, OTM -> 0. - `gamma` [Greek] Gamma: Rate of change of delta per point of underlying (2nd derivative of premium); always positive for long options.
