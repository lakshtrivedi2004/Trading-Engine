# DuPont Model

RoE = Net profit margin x Asset turnover x Financial leverage; reveals quality of returns.

## Formula
- **roe**: `(net_profit/net_sales) * (net_sales/avg_total_assets) * (avg_total_assets/avg_equity)`

## Example
ARBL 9.2% x 1.75 x 1.61 ~ 25.9%.
