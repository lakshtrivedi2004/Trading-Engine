# Cash Flow Statement

True cash generated/consumed across operating, investing and financing activities; net cash flow ties to balance sheet cash.

## Formula
- **net_cash_flow**: `CFO + CFI + CFF`

## Facts
- Revenue can include credit sales not yet collected - cash flow shows real liquidity.
- Liabilities up -> cash up; assets up -> cash down.
- Positive cash from operations is a sign of financial well-being; investors should focus on CFO.
- Healthy investing outflow signals expansion.
