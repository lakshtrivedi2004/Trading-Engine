# SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)

India's securities market regulator - develops exchanges, protects retail investors, regulates participants and intermediaries.

## Facts
- Ensures exchanges, brokers, corporates act fairly; prevents manipulation by large investors; protects retail.
- Regulates CRAs (CRISIL, ICRA, CARE), debenture trustees, depositories (NSDL, CDSL), DPs, FIIs, merchant bankers, AMCs, PMS (min Rs 25 lakh at the time), brokers/sub-brokers.
- Has final say on whether a company can go public.
- Publishes entity rules in its 'Legal Framework' section.
