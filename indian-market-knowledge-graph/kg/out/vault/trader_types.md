# Trader and Investor Types

Traders (short view, unbiased long/short) vs investors (multi-year holding).

## Facts
- Day trader - opens and closes intraday, no overnight risk, ~5-6 stocks/day.
- Scalper - very large size, very short hold, tiny per-share profit (e.g. 10,000 sh x Rs 0.10).
- Swing trader - holds days to weeks.
- Growth investor - companies growing on industry/macro trends.
- Value investor - good companies beaten down by short-term sentiment (L&T 1200->690->1440).
- Traders and investors differ on risk-taking ability and holding period.
