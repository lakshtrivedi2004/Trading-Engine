# Full Medical Disclosure (Non-disclosure risk)

Declare every pre-existing condition and habit directly to the insurer on the proposal form - never rely on an agent's verbal assurance. Non-disclosure lets the insurer repudiate the claim or cancel the policy; a loading charge and a waiting period are far better than a rejected claim.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **rule**: `disclose all medical history, medication, smoking/alcohol on the form itself`
