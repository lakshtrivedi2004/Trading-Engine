# Simple vs Compound Interest

Simple interest only on principal; compound interest on principal plus accumulated interest.

## Formula
- **simple**: `P * r * t`
- **compound_value**: `P * (1 + r) ** n`
- **cagr**: `(end/start) ** (1/n) - 1`

## Example
1L at 10% for 5 yrs - simple +50k; compound +61,051.
