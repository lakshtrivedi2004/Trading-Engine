# Short Position (Shorting)

Sell first (borrowed), buy back later; bearish; profit if price falls, loss if it rises.

## Facts
- Exchange handles borrowing/returning in the background.
- Multi-day shorts are best done in the derivatives market (equity cash shorts are intraday).
