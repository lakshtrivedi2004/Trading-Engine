# CAPM - Cost of Equity

The return equity holders expect is the risk-free rate plus beta times the market risk premium. Proxy Rf with the 10-year G-sec yield (CCIL/RBI); the long-term market return Rm is ~8-12%.

## Formula
- **re**: `Rf + beta * (Rm - Rf)`
- **risk_premium**: `beta * (Rm - Rf)`

## Example
Rf 7.4586%, beta 1.3, Rm 8.5% -> Re 8.81%.
