# Investing Principles (Finale)

Expect realistic 15-18% CAGR; long-term approach; buy investable-grade attributes; character over numbers; ignore TV noise and apply checklist; respect margin of safety; avoid IPOs (usually overpriced) unless 3-stage research; keep learning.

## Facts
- Recommended books - Essays of Warren Buffett; Little Book that Beats the Market (Greenblatt); Little Book of Valuation (Damodaran); Little Book that Builds Wealth (Dorsey).
