# Dow Theory

Charles Dow's framework (compiled by Hamilton) - 9 tenets on trends, confirmation and market phases.

## Facts
- Indices discount everything.
- Three trends - primary (1 to several years), secondary (counter-trend corrections, weeks to months), minor (daily noise).
- All indices must confirm each other (Nifty, midcap, smallcap move together).
- Volume must confirm - in uptrend volume rises on up moves, falls on dips; opposite in downtrend.
- Sideways markets can substitute for secondary trends.
- Close is the most sacred price.
- Before going long on a pattern, check primary trend; trading against a secondary counter-trend needs caution.
