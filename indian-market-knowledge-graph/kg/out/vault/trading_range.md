# Trading Range (Sideways Market)

Price oscillates between fixed support and resistance for weeks to years; buy near the bottom, short near the top.

## Facts
- Causes - no fundamental triggers, or waiting for a big announcement (short-lived).
- Longer range duration -> wider range.
