# Annual Report

Official yearly disclosure (data as of 31 March) - financial highlights, chairman's message, MD&A, 10-yr highlights, directors' report, corporate governance, financial statements, notice.

## Facts
- Prefer AR over media websites (reclassification can distort).
- MD&A is the most important section - macro, industry, segment performance, outlook; compare with peers' ARs.
- Check chairman's realism (e.g. claims 10% growth when history is 4-5% = red flag).
- Standalone vs consolidated statements - prefer consolidated (includes subsidiaries).
- Line items reference notes/schedules with details.
