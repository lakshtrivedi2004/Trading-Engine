# Crude Oil - Drivers and Ecosystem

2014-16 crash ($110 -> $28) driven by US shale supply, OPEC failing to cut, China slowdown, and heavy shorts; oil-producer breakevens (Russia needs ~$105) and fiscal stress. Upstream (ONGC, Cairn, RIL, Oil India) benefits from high oil; downstream OMCs (BPCL, HPCL, IOC) benefit from low oil; midstream wants stability.

## Facts
- WTI (API 39.6, sulphur 0.26%) is lighter/sweeter than Brent (API ~38, 0.37%); Brent is the global benchmark.
- Weekly US EIA inventory - rising inventory bearish for oil/upstream; falling inventory bullish.
- Oil and the US dollar index are generally inversely related (not always).
- For India, low oil eases subsidy, fiscal deficit and inflation but can hurt exports to oil economies.
