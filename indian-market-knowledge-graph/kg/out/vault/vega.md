# Vega

Change in premium per 1% change in volatility; positive for long calls and puts; impact largest with more time to expiry and for OTM options.

## Facts
- 30 days to expiry, vol 15->30% moved premium ~95%; 15 days ~50%; 5 days ~47%.
- Buy options when vol is expected to rise; sell when expected to fall.
- High IV raises cost and lowers profit potential of debit spreads (bull call spread 72 cost at 20% IV vs 82 at 35%).
