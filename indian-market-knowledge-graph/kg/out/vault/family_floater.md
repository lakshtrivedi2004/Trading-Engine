# Family Floater Policy

One shared sum insured for self, spouse and children at a discount to individual plans; risks within a family are correlated, so the shared cover can run out. Children must exit after an age limit; parents usually excluded. Online discounts are small (<5% first year); real discounts come from wellness programmes, multi-year tenure or floaters.
