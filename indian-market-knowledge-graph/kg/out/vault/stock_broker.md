# Stock Broker

SEBI-registered trading member of an exchange; your gateway to markets via a trading account.

## Facts
- Services - market access, margins, dealing/software support, contract notes, fund transfers, back office.
- Access modes - call & trade, web, trading terminal (e.g. Zerodha Kite).
- Charges brokerage for services.
