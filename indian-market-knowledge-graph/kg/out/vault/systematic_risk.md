# Systematic vs Unsystematic Risk

Unsystematic (company-specific - revenue/margin decline, leverage, misconduct) is diversifiable (~21 stocks enough); systematic (GDP, rates, inflation, fiscal deficit, geopolitics) is not diversifiable but can be hedged.
