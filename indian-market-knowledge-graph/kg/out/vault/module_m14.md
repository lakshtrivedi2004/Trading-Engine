# M14 - Personal Finance - Insurance

Health insurance as the first step of a financial plan (authored by Ditto) - honest disclosure, sizing the cover, co-payment and room-rent caps, pre-existing disease waiting periods and loading, specific-illness waiting periods, permanent exclusions, sub-limits and hospital blacklists, floaters, group and employer plans, a gimmick-or-not verdict on common features, claim settlement vs incurred claims ratio, and porting. Indirectly relevant to bots - it protects the trader's capital base, so drawdowns are never forced by medical emergencies.

## Caveats
- Product features, waiting periods and IRDAI rules are as of ~2021-22; verify current IRDAI regulations (e.g. moratorium/waiting-period reforms) before relying on numbers.
