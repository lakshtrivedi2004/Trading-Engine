# Interest Tax Shield / Effective Cost of Debt

Interest is tax-deductible, so its after-tax cost is lower; add back interest*(1-t) in FCFF and use the after-tax cost of debt in WACC.

## Formula
- **effective_cost_of_debt**: `kd * (1 - tax_rate)`

## Example
Interest 70 at 25% tax -> add back 52.5; kd 8% at 30% tax -> 5.6%.
