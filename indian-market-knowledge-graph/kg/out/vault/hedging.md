# Hedging

Taking an offsetting position to insulate an existing position from adverse moves (like a fence around a garden).

## Formula
- **recovery_needed**: `1/(1 - loss) - 1`

## Facts
- Why hedge rather than hold or sell - a 25% fall needs a 33.3% rise to recover; selling and re-buying requires timing, loses LTCG benefit and adds costs.
- Single stock hedge - short equal quantity of its futures; position becomes market-neutral.
- Stocks without futures or small positions - use beta-adjusted Nifty futures; positions smaller than one Nifty lot need options.
