# Discount Rate = Risk-free + Risk Premium

Opportunity cost used for PV/FV decisions = risk-free (government bond of matching tenor) + risk premium (~1.5-2%). Money today is worth more than money tomorrow.

## Example
Land sold for 7.5 Cr in 15 yrs at 9% discount = 2.06 Cr today; 2L lump-sum scheme returning 4.5L in 15 yrs is worse than 7.5% G-sec (5.92L).
