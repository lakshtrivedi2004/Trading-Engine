# Risk Management (overview)

Successful trading is ~80% money management, 20% strategy (Mark Douglas). Risk differs for single positions, multiple positions and portfolios; diversify to reduce unsystematic risk, hedge to reduce systematic risk; no position is ever 'safe'.

## Example
Satyam (Jan 2009) - company-specific fraud crashed the stock while the index fell far less - unsystematic risk.
