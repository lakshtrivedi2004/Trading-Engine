# Spot Market vs Futures Market

Spot = regular cash market; futures price moves in tandem with spot. Spot delivery buying is unleveraged, no time pressure, but T+settlement; futures are leveraged and time-bound.

## Example
TCS 2362->2519 over 9 days - spot 42 shares +5.79%; futures 2 lots on 82,670 margin +47%.
