# M08 - Currency and Commodity Futures

Currency history, forex market, pairs/quotes/pips, RBI reference rate and cross rates, macro drivers (trade deficit, rates, inflation, GDP, central bank stance), Brexit, carry trade and interest rate parity, USDINR/EURINR/GBPINR/JPYINR futures & options, calendar spreads, correlation, seasonality test, TA on currencies; MCX commodities - gold (London fix, import parity, drivers), silver, crude oil (2014-16 crash, value chain, WTI vs Brent, inventories, USD link, mini-big arbitrage), copper, aluminium, lead, nickel, agri (monsoons, cardamom, mentha), natural gas, Sumitomo and Amaranth lessons, commodity options (Black-76, CTM, devolvement). (Interest rate futures were announced but not covered.)

## Caveats
- Contract specs, lot sizes, margins and trading hours are from 2016-17 and change frequently (e.g. USDINR lot size, MCX contract variants, option expiries). Verify current exchange specs.
