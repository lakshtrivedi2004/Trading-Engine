# Profit & Loss Statement (Income Statement)

Revenue, expenses, tax and EPS for a period (year/quarter); top line = revenue, bottom line = PAT.

## Formula
- **net_revenue_from_ops**: `sale of products (net of excise) + services + other operating revenue`
- **total_revenue**: `revenue_from_operations + other_income`
- **pbt**: `total_revenue - total_expenses - exceptional_items`
- **pat**: `pbt - taxes`
- **eps**: `pat / shares_outstanding`

## Facts
- Other income (interest, dividends, royalty) should be small; large other income is a red flag.
- Depreciation spreads tangible asset cost over useful life; amortisation for intangibles; P&L doesn't show the actual capex outflow (cash flow does).
- Exceptional items are one-off.
- Negative 'change in inventories' means more produced than sold.
