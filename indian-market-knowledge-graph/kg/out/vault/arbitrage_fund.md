# Arbitrage Funds

At least 65% in hedged arbitrage (mainly cash-futures basis capture, also cross-exchange), rest in debt; equity taxation but debt-like returns (5-7%) - the real arbitrage is tax; not risk-free (debt defaults e.g. DHFL hit, slow recovery); check debt quality and no unhedged equity.

## Example
Maruti spot 5714.4 vs futures 5735.6 -> lock 21.2 basis by expiry.
