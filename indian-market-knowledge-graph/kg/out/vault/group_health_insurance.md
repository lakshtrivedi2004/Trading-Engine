# Group and Employer Health Insurance

Customised contracts for a group via a master policyholder (bank, employer). Cheaper and often with no waiting periods (invaluable for those with serious PEDs), but pricing is repriced yearly at the insurer's discretion, features are set by the master policyholder, and cover ends if you leave the job or the group dissolves (re-underwriting then). Keep a personal policy alongside an employer cover.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **rule**: `employer cover + personal base policy (or a super top-up aligned to the employer cover)`
