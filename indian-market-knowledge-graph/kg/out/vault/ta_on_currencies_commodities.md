# TA Applies Equally to Currencies and Commodities

Fundamental analysis of currencies/commodities is complex (two economies, global supply-demand), so price action/TA is the practical approach for short-term (2-3 day) trades; same patterns (piercing, marubozu, S&R, EMAs) apply.

## Example
Gold - long-term view from 2-yr chart (primary trend up, double bottom 2015); short-term entry above resistance 30956 (also 9/21 EMA), target 31418, SL 30700, RRR 1.8.
