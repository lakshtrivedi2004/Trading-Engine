# Options Trading Lessons from Case Studies

Prefer near-ATM strikes in stock options for liquidity; on 'exhaustion' views prefer selling calls (sideways likely) in 2nd half of series with SD-based strikes; author buys naked calls mainly on flag patterns (prior rally 5-10%, low-volume ~3% pullback); define entry, exit and stop for every trade.
