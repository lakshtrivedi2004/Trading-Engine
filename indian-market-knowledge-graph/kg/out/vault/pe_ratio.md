# Price to Earnings (P/E)

Price paid per rupee of earnings - the 'financial ratio superstar'.

## Formula
- **pe**: `price / eps`

## Facts
- Author avoids buying stocks above 25-30x earnings regardless of sector.
- Earnings can be manipulated - watch frequent accounting policy changes, low depreciation provisions, earnings rising without cash flow and sales.

## Example
ARBL 661/21.49 = 30.76x.
