# Correlation

-1..+1 measure of co-movement of two series; compute on returns (stationary) not prices (trending); says direction tendency, not magnitude.

## Formula
- **corr**: `CORREL(returns_a, returns_b)`

## Example
Nifty vs USDINR -0.12 (2015); Gold vs Silver intraday 0.7, EOD ~0.8.
