# EBITDA and EBITDA Margin

Operating profitability excluding other income, finance cost and D&A; reflects management efficiency.

## Formula
- **ebitda**: `(total_revenue - other_income) - (total_expenses - finance_cost - depreciation)`
- **ebitda_margin**: `ebitda / (total_revenue - other_income)`

## Example
ARBL FY14 EBITDA 560 Cr on 3436 Cr = 16.3%; 4-yr EBITDA CAGR 21%.
