# Price to Book Value (P/BV)

Price relative to book (liquidation) value per share.

## Formula
- **bvps**: `(share_capital + reserves - revaluation_reserves) / shares`
- **pb**: `price / bvps`

## Example
ARBL BV 79.8, P/BV 8.3x.
