# Operating (Activity / Management) Ratios

Efficiency of converting assets into revenue.

## Formula
- **fixed_asset_turnover**: `operating_revenue / avg_net_fixed_assets_incl_cwip`
- **working_capital**: `current_assets - current_liabilities`
- **working_capital_turnover**: `revenue / avg_working_capital`
- **total_asset_turnover**: `operating_revenue / avg_total_assets`
- **inventory_turnover**: `COGS / avg_inventory`
- **inventory_days**: `365 / inventory_turnover`
- **receivable_turnover**: `revenue / avg_receivables`
- **dso**: `365 / receivable_turnover`
- **cogs**: `materials + stock-in-trade purchases + stores & spares + power & fuel`

## Caveats
- High inventory turnover could reflect low production capacity - investigate funding
- raw material access
- scalability.

## Example
ARBL FAT 5.59, WCT 5.11, TAT 1.75, inventory turnover 7.8 (~47 days), receivables 8.24x (~44 days).
