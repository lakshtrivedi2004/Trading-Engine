# Option Jargon (strike, underlying, premium, expiry, exercise, settlement, option chain)

Strike = anchor price; underlying = spot; premium = price paid by buyer to writer (never fixed, driven by Greeks); expiry = last valid date (3 monthly series per source); exercise = claiming the right (European - only on expiry); option chain lists all strikes with LTP, bid/ask, OI, volume, IV.

## Facts
- CE = European call
- PE = European put. All Indian options are European (American stock options were discontinued).
