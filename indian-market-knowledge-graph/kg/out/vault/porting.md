# Porting a Health Policy

Move a policy to a new insurer while carrying over waiting-period credit. Apply 45-60 days before renewal and prove continuous coverage with documents; credit applies only up to the old sum insured (plus bonus) - extra cover serves fresh waiting periods; the new insurer may re-underwrite. Verdict - not a gimmick; usually better than buying fresh.

## Parameters
- **window_days_before_renewal**: `45-60`
