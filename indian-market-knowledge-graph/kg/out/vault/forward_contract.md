# Forward Contract

OTC private agreement to exchange an asset at a fixed price on a future date; settled physically or in cash.

## Facts
- Risks - liquidity (finding counterparty), default/counterparty, regulatory (unregulated), rigidity (cannot exit mid-way).
- Buyer profits if price rises above agreed price; seller profits if it falls; linear payoff.
