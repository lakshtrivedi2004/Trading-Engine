# Chart Types (Line, Bar, Candlestick)

Line chart plots closes only (trend view, little info); bar chart shows OHLC with left tick open/right tick close (hard to read); Japanese candlestick is the default.

## Facts
- Bar length (high-low) is the range.
- Candlesticks originated with rice trader Homma Munehisa (18th c.), popularised in the West by Steve Nison.
