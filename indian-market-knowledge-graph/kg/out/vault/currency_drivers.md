# Fundamental Drivers of Currencies

Pair moves on the dominant of two economies' factors - exports strengthen domestic currency, imports weaken it; narrowing trade deficit is positive; higher interest rates attract capital (carry) and strengthen currency; hawkish stance strengthens, dovish weakens; high inflation -> expected hawkishness -> stronger currency (per source); higher GDP growth -> stronger currency.

## Facts
- Major events (Brexit Jun 2016 - GBP -8.6%, 31-yr low) create extreme moves; uncertainty is what markets hate.
- 'When in confusion, do nothing' is itself a good trade.
- FII inflows into Indian equities strengthen INR - USDINR and Nifty are inversely correlated (~-0.12, 2015).

## Caveats
- Simplified textbook causality; relationships are unstable.
