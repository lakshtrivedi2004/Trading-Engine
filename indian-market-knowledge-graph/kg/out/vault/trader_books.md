# Trader's Balance Sheet, P&L and Books of Account

Personal balance sheet (assets - liabilities = net worth), P&L (trading revenue minus trading expenses; salary excluded), and two books - bank book (annotated statements + bills) and trading book (broker P&L, ledger, contract notes); update quarterly.
