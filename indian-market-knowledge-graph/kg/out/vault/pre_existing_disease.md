# Pre-existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period and Loading

Declared conditions (diabetes, BP, etc.) trigger a PED waiting period (usually 2-4 years) during which related complications aren't covered, and/or a loading charge on the premium (typically 10-20%, up to ~150% for multiple illnesses); insurers may also reject the proposal.

## Parameters
- **ped_waiting_years**: `2-4`
- **loading**: `10-20% typical, up to 150%`
