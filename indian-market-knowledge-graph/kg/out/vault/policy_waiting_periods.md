# Waiting Periods (Initial, Specific Illness, PED)

Three layers - initial 30 days (accidents only); specific-illness list (cataract, stones, deviated septum, hernia, joint replacement etc. - treatments that can be deferred), typically 2 years; PED waiting 2-4 years. Dengue, malaria, fractures, most cancers and heart conditions are covered after 30 days.

## Parameters
- **initial_days**: `30`
- **specific_illness_years**: `2`
- **ped_years**: `2-4`
