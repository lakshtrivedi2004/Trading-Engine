# What Moves Stock Prices

Prices move on news/events (company, industry, economy) and expectations, and on demand-supply imbalance even without news.

## Facts
- Good news -> buyers lift offers repeatedly -> price rally (Infosys new CEO example 3000->3016 in 5 min).
- Industry-wide news (e.g. NASSCOM cutting IT budget outlook) hits all sector stocks.
- Large liquid stocks move even without news; illiquid small caps may barely move.
- Macro/political events (e.g. 2014 election) move the whole market.

## Bot use
News/event sentiment and sector contagion features; liquidity filter - illiquid names don't respond.
