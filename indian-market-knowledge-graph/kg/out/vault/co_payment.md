# Co-payment Clause

You pay a fixed % of every claim (e.g. 20%) in exchange for a lower premium - gives you 'skin in the game'. Usually a bad trade.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **avoid_unless**: `mandatory, or buying for elderly with pre-existing diseases where it cuts premium sharply`

## Example
20% co-pay saved Rs 1,800/yr on a Rs 7,000 premium, but a Rs 2 lakh claim cost Rs 40,000 out of pocket (~22 years of savings).
