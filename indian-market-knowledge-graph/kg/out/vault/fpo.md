# Follow-on Public Offer (FPO)

Post-listing public issue of fresh (dilutive) shares via DRHP + book building, used to fund new projects; rare since OFS (2012).

## Example
Engineers India FPO Feb 2014, band 145-150, 3x oversubscribed, lower band 4.2% below market.
