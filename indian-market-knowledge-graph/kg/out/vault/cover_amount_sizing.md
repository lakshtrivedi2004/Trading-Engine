# Choosing the Sum Insured

Most expensive treatments rarely cross ~Rs 20 lakh; a Rs 10-20 lakh base cover (boosted by no-claim bonus and restoration) handles most cases. Rs 5 lakh may be inadequate within a decade given 6-7% medical inflation. A smaller cover is fine for the elderly or if you can't sustain the premium - lapsing a policy is the worst outcome. For rare huge bills, add a super top-up instead of buying a huge base cover.

## Parameters
- **base_cover_inr**: `10-20 lakh`
- **medical_inflation**: `6-7%/yr`
- **premium_inflation**: `4-6%/yr`
