# Margin of Safety

Benjamin Graham's principle - buy only at a discount to intrinsic value; author takes a further >= 30% discount to the lower value band.

## Formula
- **buy_price**: `IV * 0.9 * 0.7`

## Facts
- Bear markets offer such discounts - keep cash ready.

## Example
ARBL IV 368 -> lower band 331 -> buy ~230 with conviction.
