# Agri Commodities and Monsoons

2/3 of Indian arable land is rain-fed; SW monsoon (Jun-Sep) -> Kharif crops (rice, pulses, cotton, cardamom); NE monsoon (Dec-Mar) -> Rabi crops (wheat, mustard). Track monsoon forecasts and sowing data before trading agri - author lost his account long pepper ahead of a bumper monsoon.
