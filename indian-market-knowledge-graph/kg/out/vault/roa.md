# Return on Assets (RoA)

Efficiency of asset use, adding back after-tax interest (tax shield) since lenders fund assets.

## Formula
- **roa**: `(net_income + interest*(1 - tax_rate)) / avg_total_assets`

## Example
ARBL (367.4 + 4.76)/1955 = 19.03%.
