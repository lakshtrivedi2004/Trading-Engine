# Scalping

Large-size trades held minutes on 1m/5m charts for small moves; for seasoned traders.

## Facts
- Use candlestick pattern + volume from checklist; RRR 0.5-0.75 acceptable.
- Only liquid stocks; quick to book losses; watch bid-ask and global markets (e.g. Hang Seng); low-cost broker essential; don't over-leverage; stop trading on bad days.

## Example
Short 1000 Infosys 3980 at 10:15, cover 3976 at 10:25 -> Rs 2653 net.
