# Stock Market Index (Nifty / Sensex)

Basket of large, liquid stocks acting as a barometer of the market/economy; Nifty (NSE) and Sensex (BSE).

## Facts
- Uses - information, benchmarking performance, trading (via derivatives), portfolio hedging.
- Index up = participants optimistic about future; down = pessimistic.
- Weights by free-float market capitalisation; stocks must keep qualifying or get replaced.
- A stock's weight is the share of index movement attributable to it (e.g. ITC 7.6% in 2014).
- Sectoral indices - Bank Nifty, CNX IT etc.
- Majority of traders trade the index.

## Bot use
Benchmark strategy returns against Nifty; use index as regime/market-trend filter; hedge with index futures.
