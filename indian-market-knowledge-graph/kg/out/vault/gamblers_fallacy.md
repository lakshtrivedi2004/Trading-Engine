# Gambler's Fallacy

Believing a streak must end - increasing size after losses ('next one will win') or cutting size after wins; each independent trade has the same odds. Antidote - systematic position sizing.

## Example
Author lost 3 poker buy-ins chasing losses; with sizing discipline he lasted and won.
