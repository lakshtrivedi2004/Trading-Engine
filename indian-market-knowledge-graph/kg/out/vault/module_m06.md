# M06 - Option Strategies

Behavioural warning on gambling with options; bull call/put spreads, call ratio back spread, bear call ladder, synthetic long and put-call-parity arbitrage, bear put/call spreads, put ratio back spread, long/short straddle and strangle, max pain and PCR. (The source lists further strategies - straps, strips, iron condor, butterfly, box, synthetic put, bull put ladder - but does not cover them.)
