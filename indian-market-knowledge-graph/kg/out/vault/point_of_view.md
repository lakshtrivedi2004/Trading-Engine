# Point of View (POV) and Instrument Selection

Success requires a researched directional view (via FA, TA, quant) plus choosing the right instrument to express it.

## Facts
- Methods - Fundamental, Technical, Quantitative analysis, outside views (avoid relying on outside views).
- Long-term very bullish -> delivery equity; short-term (1 week) bullish -> futures; bullish with limited risk -> options.
- Bullish can be expressed via spot, futures, calls, puts combos or synthetic positions.

## Bot use
Map signal horizon/conviction to instrument - equity for long-term, futures for short-term directional, options for capped-risk/event trades.
