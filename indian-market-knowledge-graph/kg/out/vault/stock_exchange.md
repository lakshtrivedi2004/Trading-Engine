# Stock Exchange (NSE / BSE)

Electronic marketplace matching buyers and sellers; India's two main exchanges are NSE and BSE.

## Facts
- Exists electronically; accessed only via a registered stock broker.
- Exchange's order-matching algorithm pairs orders; seller can be one or many counterparties.
- Different opinions make a market - a trade needs an opposing view.
- Regional exchanges are being phased out.
