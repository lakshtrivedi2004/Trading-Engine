# Initial Public Offering (IPO)

First offer of a company's shares to the public, moving it from private to listed (primary -> secondary market).

## Facts
- Reasons - raise capex funds, spread promoter risk, exit for early investors, reward employees via ESOPs, visibility.
- Sequence - appoint merchant banker -> registration statement to SEBI -> SEBI nod -> DRHP -> road show -> price band -> book building -> closure -> listing day.
- DRHP contents - IPO size, shares offered, use of funds with timeline, business model, financials, MD&A, risks, management.
- Book building - investors bid within the price band; listing price usually where max bids received.
- Undersubscription signals negative sentiment; oversubscription Nx = bids/shares offered.
- Green shoe option - over-allotment up to ~15% extra shares if oversubscribed.
- Fixed price IPO has no band; cut-off price = final discovered price within band.
- Most Indian IPOs follow book building.

## Caveats
- Investors should read the DRHP; some companies list without healthy prior funding rounds.

## Bot use
Subscription multiple and anchor/VC pedigree are pre-listing sentiment features; listing day = start of secondary trading.
