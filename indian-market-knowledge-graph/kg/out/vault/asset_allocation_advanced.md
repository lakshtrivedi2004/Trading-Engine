# Asset Allocation, Sequence Risk and Diversification Vectors

Prediction is impossible (India beat US 2001-11, reversed next decade) - own a bit of all major assets. Sequence risk - an investor rarely gets average returns; bad years can cluster in your window. Diversify across real drivers (currency, market composition, panic flows - US bonds are safe havens, Indian bonds aren't; avoid bubble assets), not 'diworsification'. Simple start - Indian equity (large-cap index + mid-cap), US equity (cheap S&P 500 fund), short-term govt/PSU bonds, gold (Sovereign Gold Bonds), REITs; equal weight if unsure; stick to the plan.

## Caveats
- Financialisation raises correlations between assets (e.g. REITs vs stocks).
