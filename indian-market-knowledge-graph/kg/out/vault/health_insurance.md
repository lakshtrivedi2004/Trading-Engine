# Health Insurance (Indemnity)

Pays hospitalisation bills up to the sum insured; the core protection product. Premiums rise every year (~4-6% medical inflation adjustment plus age-band jumps, occasionally regulator-approved repricing) but cannot be raised because you claimed.

## Facts
- First 30 days - only accidental hospitalisation is covered.
- Healthcare costs inflate ~6-7%/yr.
- Premium increases are not caused by your own claims (urban myth).
