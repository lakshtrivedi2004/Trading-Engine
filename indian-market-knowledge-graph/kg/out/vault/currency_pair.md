# Currency Pairs, Quotes and Pips

Quoted as BASE/QUOTE = units of quote per 1 base; buying the pair = long base, short quote (dual view). Two-way quote bid/ask (e.g. EUR/USD 1.1269/70); a pip is the minimum move (USDINR 0.0025).

## Formula
- **pnl**: `lot_size * lots * points_moved`
- **pnl_per_tick**: `(lot_size / quotation_unit) * tick_size`

## Facts
- Pair up = base strengthens/quote weakens.
- GBP/USD is nicknamed 'Cable'.
