# Index Funds (passive investing)

Track a benchmark cheaply; active management is a zero-sum game before costs and negative after costs - SPIVA India - ~82% of large-cap funds underperformed BSE 100 over 5 yrs (2019); expense gap ~1% compounds massively; paradox of skill; closet indexing. Use index funds for large (and increasingly mid) caps; combine active and passive if desired.
