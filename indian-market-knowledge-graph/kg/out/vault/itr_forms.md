# ITR Forms

(Source era) ITR-1 salary/interest/one house; ITR-2 adds capital gains (investors); ITR-4 adds business income (traders - F&O/intraday); ITR-4S presumptive. Filing is mandatory even if tax was paid via TDS; file losses on time to carry forward.

## Caveats
- Current mapping - business income is ITR-3
- presumptive is ITR-4 (Sugam).
