# Speculator vs Trader vs Investor

Speculator bets on blind faith/outside views; trader designs and backtests trades (e.g. selling high-IV options before RBI expecting volatility drop); investor ignores short-term noise and may buy more on overreaction.

## Facts
- Doing nothing is itself a market action. A trader 'designs all his trades and not just speculates on an outcome'.
