# Mutual Funds (structure, NAV, factsheet)

Pooled vehicle - sponsor (promoter, holds licence) appoints trustee and AMC (investment manager); custodian holds assets; RTA services investors. Units issued at notional value; NAV moves with holdings. Factsheet - objective, open/close-ended, plans (direct vs regular), options (dividend/growth), riskometer.
