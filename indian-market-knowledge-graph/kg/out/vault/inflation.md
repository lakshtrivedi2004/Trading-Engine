# Inflation (CPI / WPI)

Sustained rise in general prices measured by WPI (wholesale) and CPI (retail); MOSPI publishes CPI ~2nd week monthly.

## Facts
- High inflation is a bad signal for markets.
- CPI is what matters to consumers.
