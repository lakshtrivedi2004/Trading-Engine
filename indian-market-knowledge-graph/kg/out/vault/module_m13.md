# M13 - Integrated Financial Modelling

Building an integrated 3-statement Excel model (main model - Bajaj Auto; helper models - Bata etc.) from 5 years of annual-report data - workbook layout, assumption sheet (ratio/growth + rolling averages), revenue sub-model, asset/depreciation, debt and reserves schedules, P&L and balance-sheet projections, indirect cash flow that balances the balance sheet, then valuation (relative, option-based, absolute) via FCFF/FCFE, CAPM, tax shield, WACC, terminal value and DCF to a per-share fair value.
