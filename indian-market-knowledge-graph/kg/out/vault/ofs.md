# Offer for Sale (OFS)

Exchange window for promoters to sell existing shares (no new shares) at/above a floor price; also to meet minimum public shareholding.

## Facts
- Only top 200 companies by market cap may use OFS (per source).
- Allotment settled to demat T+1.
- PSU minimum public shareholding 25% example.

## Example
NTPC OFS Aug 2017, 46.35 mn shares, floor Rs 168, fully subscribed.
