# Core-Satellite Capital Allocation

Split capital (e.g. 60/40) - core in fundamentally strong long-term holdings (expect 12-15% CAGR), satellite for active TA trading in equity/F&O (expect 10-12% absolute/yr).

## Parameters
- **core_pct**: `60`
- **satellite_pct**: `40`

## Bot use
Allocate a capped satellite bucket to algorithmic strategies while core remains long-term.
