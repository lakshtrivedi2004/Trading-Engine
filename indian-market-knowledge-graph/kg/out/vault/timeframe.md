# Timeframes and Look-back Period

Monthly (12 candles/yr), weekly (52), daily (~252), intraday 30m (~12/day), 15m (25/day), 5m (75/day); choose to filter information from noise.

## Parameters
- **daily_candles_per_year**: `252`
- **swing_lookback_months**: `[6, 12]`
- **sr_lookback_years**: `2`

## Facts
- Long-term investors - weekly/monthly; swing/positional - EOD; scalpers - 1m/5m.
- Higher timeframe signals are more reliable (15m engulfing > 5m engulfing).
- Look-back - swing trader 6-12 months; scalper ~5 days; S&R plotting at least 2 years (or 3-6 months for short-term S&R, 12-18 months for long-term S&R).
- Beginners should start with positional/swing trades (hold a few days), not day trading.
