# Four Option Positions Summary

Bullish - buy call or sell put; bearish - buy put or sell call. Buyers - limited risk, unlimited reward; sellers - limited reward, unlimited risk ('eat like a chicken, shit like an elephant' - Taleb/Das).

## Facts
- Buying to close a short (or selling to close a long) is a square-off, not a new long/short.
- P&L formulas apply when held to expiry; premiums move continuously before expiry.
