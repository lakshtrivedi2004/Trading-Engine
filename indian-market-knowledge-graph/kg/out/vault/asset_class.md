# Asset Class

A category of investment with particular risk and return characteristics - fixed income, equity, real estate, commodities (precious metals).

## Facts
- Fixed income - limited risk to principal, interest paid periodically, capital returned at maturity; ~8-11% (2014).
- Equity - no capital guarantee; Indian equities ~14-15% CAGR over 15 years; best companies >20% CAGR.
- Real estate - rental income + capital appreciation; large outlay, illiquid, no official return metric.
- Bullion (gold/silver) - ~8% CAGR over 20 years; via jewelry or ETFs.
- Risk and return go hand in hand.
- Fixed income can lose in real terms - a 9% FD with 10% inflation = -1% real return.
