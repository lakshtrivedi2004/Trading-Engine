# Expiry, Contract Months and Rollover

Three concurrent monthly contracts (current/near, mid, far); price usually near < mid < far; on expiry a new far month is listed. Rollover = closing near-month and opening next month near expiry.

## Facts
- Directional view must play out before expiry.
- Near month is most liquid, hence rollover is common.
- Rollover data - rolling longs = bullish perception, rolling shorts = bearish (perception only).
