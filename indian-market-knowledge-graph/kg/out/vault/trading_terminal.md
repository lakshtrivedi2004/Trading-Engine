# Trading Terminal / Market Watch

Broker software to view quotes (LTP, % change, OHLC, volume), market depth and place/modify orders.

## Facts
- Order book tracks orders (open/completed/rejected) and allows modify/cancel; trade book shows executed trades.
- Market depth (snap quote) shows top 5 bid/ask levels with order counts and quantities.
