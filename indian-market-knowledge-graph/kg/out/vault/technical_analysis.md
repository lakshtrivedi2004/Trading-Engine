# Technical Analysis (TA)

Research technique identifying trading opportunities from market participants' actions (price/volume) visualised as charts and patterns.

## Facts
- Best for short-term trades (minutes to weeks); use FA for long-term investments; FA investors can use TA to time entries/exits.
- Expect small but consistent profits from frequent short-term trades, not windfalls.
- Cut losses quickly; do not hold losing TA trades hoping to recover.
- Applicable to any asset with OHLCV time series - equities, commodities, FX, fixed income; indicators work identically across assets.
- TA vs FA is not a contest; prudent traders learn both.
- Assumptions - (1) markets discount everything, (2) 'how' matters more than 'why', (3) price moves in trends, (4) history tends to repeat itself.
- History repeats only when the same set of factors recurs (factor addendum).
- Indian market hours 9:15 to 15:30.
