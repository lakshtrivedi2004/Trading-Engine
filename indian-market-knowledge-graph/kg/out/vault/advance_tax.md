# Advance Tax

Business income or realised STCG requires advance tax instalments - 30% by 15 Sep, 60% by 15 Dec, 100% by 15 Mar (source; a 15 June instalment now also exists); ~12% p.a. interest on shortfall; pay on profits realised to date, claim refund if overpaid.
