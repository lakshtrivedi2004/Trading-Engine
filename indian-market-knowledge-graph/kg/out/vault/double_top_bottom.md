# Double / Triple Top and Bottom

Reversal patterns where a price level is tested twice (or thrice) with tests well spaced in time (>= ~2 weeks); triple is stronger than double.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **double_bottom**: `two lows within tolerance, separated by >= ~10 sessions with a rebound between -> bullish`
- **double_top**: `two highs within tolerance (e.g. 336 vs 332 accepted) -> bearish`

## Facts
- Best when the second test forms a candlestick pattern (e.g. shooting star on 2nd top).
