# Company Funding Lifecycle

Seed (promoter + angels) -> Series A (VC) -> Series B -> Series C (Private Equity) -> IPO; valuation rises as revenue/profit validate business.

## Formula
- **implied_valuation**: `investment_amount / stake_fraction`

## Facts
- Angel investors invest pre-revenue, take maximum risk (as much as promoter), small amounts.
- Seed fund becomes initial share capital in the company's bank account.
- Face value - nominal denomination per share (e.g. Rs 10).
- Authorized shares - total shares available; issued shares are a subset allotted to holders.
- VC invests early-stage, smaller amounts; PE invests large sums in mature firms and takes board seats.
- Capex can be funded by internal accruals, equity (VC/PE/IPO) or debt (loans/bonds).
- Implied valuation = investment / stake (e.g. 7 Cr for 14% => 50 Cr).
- Credible prior VC/PE rounds are a quality signal for IPO companies.
