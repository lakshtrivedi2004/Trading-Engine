# GDP, Real vs Nominal Growth, and Market Cap

GDP = total economic output; real growth = nominal - inflation; India ~$2.6-2.9T (5th/6th, 2020); as GDP grows, aggregate market cap tends to grow - basis for long-term equity investing.

## Formula
- **real_gdp_growth**: `nominal_growth - inflation`
