# Permanent Exclusions, Sub-limits, Non-medical Items, Blacklisted Hospitals

Insurer defences against abuse - permanent exclusions (substance/alcohol-induced illness, cosmetic treatment, external congenital conditions); disease-wise sub-limits (e.g. cardiac Rs 2.5L, knee Rs 2.75L, cataract Rs 50k regardless of cover) - avoid policies with them; non-medical consumables excluded; only 'just and reasonable' treatment per medical council norms paid; blacklisted hospitals can be refused.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **check_before_buying**: `no disease-wise sub-limits; read permanent exclusion list; check hospital blacklist; get a second opinion for expensive procedures`

## Example
Rs 10 lakh cover, slipped-disc bill Rs 4.36 lakh, insurer paid only Rs 2 lakh due to a sub-limit.
