# Compounding Effect

Reinvesting gains lets money grow on itself; the longer you stay invested, the faster absolute wealth grows.

## Formula
- **future_value**: `P * (1 + r) ** n`

## Example
Rs 100 at 20% - took ~6 yrs to reach 300, next 300 in just 4 yrs; reinvesting gives 173 vs 160 when withdrawing profits after 3 yrs.
