# Return on Equity (RoE)

Profit per unit of shareholders' equity; key investable attribute. Top Indian companies average 14-16%; author prefers >18-20% (checklist >25%) without high debt.

## Formula
- **roe**: `net_profit / avg_shareholders_equity`

## Caveats
- Debt inflates RoE (pizza oven example 25% -> 31% -> 50% as debt rises); always inspect with DuPont and debt levels.
