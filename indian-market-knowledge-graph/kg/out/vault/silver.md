# Silver

Precious + industrial metal (demand ~1170 mn oz, supply ~1040 mn oz - slight deficit); 'bullion twin' of gold (corr ~0.7 intraday, ~0.8 EOD) - candidate for gold-silver pair trading; weak correlation with crude.
