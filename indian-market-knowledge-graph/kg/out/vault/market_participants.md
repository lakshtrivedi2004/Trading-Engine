# Market Participants

Domestic retail, NRI/OCI, domestic institutions (e.g. LIC), domestic AMCs (mutual funds), Foreign Institutional Investors (FIIs).

## Facts
- FIIs transact large amounts so their activity moves market sentiment.
- Everyone's agenda is to make profitable transactions; greed/fear drive unfair practices, hence regulation.
