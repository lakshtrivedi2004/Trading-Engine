# Balance Sheet

Cumulative (flow-basis) statement of assets, liabilities and shareholders' equity; Assets = Liabilities + Equity.

## Formula
- **equation**: `assets = shareholders_funds + non_current_liabilities + current_liabilities`
- **equity**: `share_capital + reserves + surplus`
- **share_capital**: `face_value * shares`
- **shares_outstanding**: `share_capital / face_value`
- **net_block**: `gross_block - accumulated_depreciation`

## Facts
- Shareholders' funds appear on liability side because the company owes them to shareholders.
- Reserves - capital, securities premium, general; surplus accumulates PAT, pays dividends.
- Non-current liabilities (>12 months) - long-term borrowings, deferred tax liability (depreciation timing), long-term provisions (employee benefits).
- Current liabilities - short-term borrowings (working capital), trade payables, other, short-term provisions.
- Non-current assets - tangible/intangible fixed assets, CWIP, investments, long-term loans/advances.
- Current assets - inventory, trade receivables, cash & equivalents, short-term loans/advances.
- No debt is good but ask why (can't borrow? not expanding?).
- P&L links - PAT -> surplus; credit sales -> receivables; purchases -> payables/inventory; capex -> depreciation; debt -> finance cost.
