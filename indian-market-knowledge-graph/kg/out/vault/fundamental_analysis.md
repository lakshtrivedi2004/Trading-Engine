# Fundamental Analysis (FA)

Holistic study of a business to identify investment-grade companies for long-term (3-5+ yr) wealth creation, separating business performance from short-term price noise.

## Facts
- Wealth creators (Infosys, TCS, Page, Eicher, Bosch, Nestle, TTK Prestige) compounded >20% CAGR for 10+ years; wealth destroyers include Suzlon, Reliance Power, Sterling Biotech.
- At 20% CAGR money doubles in ~3.5 years (rule of 72).
- Skills needed - reading financial statements, understanding business in its industry, basic arithmetic.
- Tools - annual report, industry data, news, Excel.
- TA and FA should coexist (Eicher flat 2006-2010 then multi-bagger - short-term trades fill the gap).
