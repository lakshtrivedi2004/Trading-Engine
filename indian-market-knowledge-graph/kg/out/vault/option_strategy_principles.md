# Option Strategy Principles

Know a handful of strategies well and map market view to strategy; never treat options as lottery tickets ('limited risk, unlimited profit' is a silent P&L killer); professionals prefer spreads for risk visibility even at lower profit; prefer net-credit strategies; always sum deltas to know a multi-leg position's directional bias.

## Facts
- Moderate move quantified by volatility - volatile stocks 5-8%, less volatile stocks and indices < 5%.
- Delta-neutral strategies react to volatility and time ('volatility based strategies').
- Winning on options can be addictive (reflexive vs reflective brain).
