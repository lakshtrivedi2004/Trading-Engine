# Commodity Blow-ups (Sumitomo copper, Amaranth natural gas)

Sumitomo's Hamanaka cornered ~5% of world copper with leveraged LME longs; Chinese supply crashed prices -> ~$5bn loss (1995/96). Amaranth's Brian Hunter took leveraged nat-gas bets on weather, doubled down at 8:1 leverage after a 20% fall -> ~$6bn loss (2006). Lesson - risk management sits above all; never double down on leveraged losers.
