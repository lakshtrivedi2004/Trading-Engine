# Interest Coverage Ratio

Ability to service interest from operating earnings; low coverage = default risk.

## Formula
- **icr**: `EBIT / interest_expense`
- **ebit**: `EBITDA - D&A`

## Example
Jain Irrigation FY14 565.44/467.64 = 1.21x (weak).
