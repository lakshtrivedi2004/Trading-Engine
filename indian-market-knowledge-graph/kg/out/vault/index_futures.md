# Nifty (Index) Futures

Futures on the Nifty index; most liquid derivative in India.

## Facts
- Benefits vs stock futures - diversified (no unsystematic risk), hard to manipulate, very liquid (impact cost ~0.0125%), lower margins (12-15% vs up to 45-60%), broad economic call, TA works better, lower volatility (~16-17% vs 30%+ for stocks).
- Lot size changes over time (25 in 2015; verify current).

## Bot use
Preferred instrument for systematic index strategies and portfolio hedges.
