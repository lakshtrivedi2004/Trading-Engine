# Health Insurance Features - Gimmick or Not

Ditto's verdicts on marketed features.

## Facts
- Network hospitals (cashless) - not a gimmick.
- AYUSH cover - slightly gimmicky (only in government-certified AYUSH hospitals with inpatient admission).
- Restoration benefit - not a gimmick, but buyer beware.
- Pre/post hospitalisation (e.g. 30/60 days to 60/180 days) - near-universal, nothing extraordinary.
- Day care (<24h procedures like chemo, dialysis, appendicitis) - not a gimmick; make sure it's covered.
- No claim bonus - not a gimmick.
- Domiciliary cover - slightly gimmicky (needs inability to move / no bed, doctor certification, 72h+).
- Consumables add-on (2-10% of bill) - not a gimmick but touch-and-go; better if bundled with inflation protection.
- Critical illness - slightly gimmicky (narrow severity definitions; a super top-up or term plan does better).
- Top-up - highly gimmicky (deductible applies per hospitalisation).
- Super top-up - not a gimmick, but align policy dates.
- Claim settlement ratio - not a gimmick, but needs context (with ICR).
- Porting - not a gimmick.
