# Trading System

A defined, quantified, backtestable process that takes market inputs, applies fixed logic and outputs a trade decision; gut, tips, TV, broker calls and ad-hoc 'systematic deduction' are not systems. Not a holy grail - you design the logic once and follow it; backtesting is mandatory and must be redone as markets change.
