# Standard Deviation and Variance

Variance = mean of squared deviations from mean; SD = sqrt(variance); measures dispersion/consistency.

## Formula
- **variance**: `sum((x - mean)^2)/n`
- **sd**: `sqrt(variance)`

## Example
Billy SD 1.79 vs Mike SD 11.18 - Billy more consistent despite lower mean.
