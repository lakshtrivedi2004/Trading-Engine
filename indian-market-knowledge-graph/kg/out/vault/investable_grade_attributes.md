# Investable-Grade Attributes (Qualitative + Quantitative)

Qualitative (weighted more by author) - management background, ethics, corporate governance, minority shareholder treatment, clandestine promoter share transactions, related-party transactions, promoter salaries, operator activity, significant shareholders, political affiliation, promoter lifestyle. Quantitative - profit/margin/earnings growth, expenses, efficiency, pricing power, taxes, dividends, cash flows, debt, working capital, assets, ratios.

## Facts
- A red flag in any qualitative factor overrides great margins - the market eventually punishes poor governance.
- Sector-specific metrics matter (retail - stores, sales/sq ft; oil & gas - reserves, exploration costs).
- Hold as long as attributes remain intact; sell when they crumble (not because of price alone).
