# Government Finances and Fiscal Deficit

Revenue = tax (direct - personal/corporate income tax; indirect - GST) + non-tax (PSU dividends, disinvestment); expenditure = revenue (subsidies, salaries, interest) + capital (infrastructure). Fiscal deficit = expenditure - revenue; India FY19 ~3.3% of GDP (govt aims < 4%); higher capex share and tax-to-GDP are positive signals.

## Formula
- **fiscal_deficit_pct**: `(total_expenditure - total_revenue) / GDP`
