# M07 - Markets and Taxation

Indian income tax basics for market participants (as of FY 2014-15/2015-16) - FY vs AY, slabs, classifying income (LTCG, STCG, speculative and non-speculative business income), investor taxation incl. indexation and FIFO, trader taxation, loss set-off/carry-forward, tax-loss harvesting, BTST treatment, advance tax, business turnover and tax audit (44AB/44AD), personal balance sheet/P&L/books, ITR forms.

## Caveats
- ALL RATES/THRESHOLDS ARE FROM THE SOURCE ERA (FY2014-16). Indian tax law has changed materially since (e.g. LTCG on listed equity reintroduced in 2018; STCG/LTCG rates revised in July 2024; debt MF and indexation rules changed 2023-24; business-income ITR is now ITR-3; audit thresholds changed). Treat as conceptual framework; verify current law before use.
