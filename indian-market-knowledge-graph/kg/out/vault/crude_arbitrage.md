# Crude Big vs Mini Arbitrage

Same underlying, different lot sizes - if prices diverge, buy the cheaper and sell the dearer in equal notional (10 minis vs 1 big) to lock the spread until convergence.

## Example
Crude 3221 vs Mini 3217 -> short 1 big, long 10 minis -> +4 pts regardless of convergence level.
