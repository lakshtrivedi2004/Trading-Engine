# 3-Stage Equity Research

(1) Understand the business via Q&A from 5 years of annual reports (~15 hrs, summarise on one page; stop on any red flag); (2) apply the checklist; (3) valuation via DCF and margin of safety. Stages are sequential.

## Facts
- Key questions - what the company does, promoters' background, products, plants & capacity utilisation, raw material dependency (govt-regulated?), clients, competitors (monopoly/duopoly/oligopoly = higher margins), major shareholders, new products/geographies, revenue mix, regulation, bankers & auditors, labour issues, entry barriers, replicability in cheap-labour countries, number of subsidiaries.
- Author prefers businesses with little competition and little government intervention.
- Bear markets move prices, not fundamentals - conviction lets you buy in bear markets.
