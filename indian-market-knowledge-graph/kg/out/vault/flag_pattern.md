# Flag Formation

Steep rally (pole) then 5-15 session low-volume correction in a parallel channel; price then resumes sharply upward - second chance to buy.

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **identify**: `sharp rally then 5-15 bar pullback within parallel lines on declining volume`
- **entry**: `break above flag channel (act fast)`

## Facts
- Pullback is retail profit-taking; smart money stays invested.
