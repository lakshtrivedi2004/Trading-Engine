# Market Phases (Accumulation, Mark-up, Distribution, Mark-down)

Cycle - smart money accumulates after steep sell-off (creates support), mark-up is a fast rally that leaves public behind, distribution when public buys near highs (creates resistance), then mark-down sell-off.

## Facts
- No two cycles are the same; can span months to years.
