# Debt Funds and Debt Risks

Companies borrow via bonds (>1 yr) and commercial paper (<1 yr), government via T-bills/G-secs; lenders face credit, interest-rate, liquidity and rating-change risk. Overnight (1-day, tri-party repo) < liquid (<=91 days) < ultra-short (3-6 m Macaulay) < low duration < short/medium duration; banking & PSU (lower credit risk); credit risk funds (avoid as retail); gilts (no credit risk, high rate risk).

## Facts
- Bond prices move inversely to yields/interest rates; modified duration ~ % NAV change per 1% rate change.
- Liquid funds are not savings accounts (Taurus, Franklin Templeton 2020 wind-up, DHFL defaults).
- Watch fund borrowing (liquidity stress), concentrated corporate exposure, sub-investment-grade paper; hold at least the fund's average maturity.
