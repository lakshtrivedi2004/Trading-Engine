# Options (overview)

Right but not obligation for the buyer (who pays premium) to buy (call) or sell (put) the underlying at a strike on/before expiry; seller/writer has the obligation. ~80% of Indian derivatives volume (source era).

## Facts
- Indian history - index futures Jun 2000, index options Jun 2001, stock options Jul 2001, stock futures Nov 2001; real liquidity from ~2006.
- Options are cash settled in India (stock options later moved to physical settlement - verify).
- Option seller has natural statistical edge - 2 of 3 scenarios (flat, adverse) favour seller.
- Most traders trade premium changes rather than hold to expiry; premiums can swing 100-350% intraday.
- Option sellers tend to hold to expiry to earn full premium.
