# Debt to Equity Ratio

Total (short + long-term) debt relative to equity; >1 indicates high leverage.

## Formula
- **de**: `total_debt / total_equity`

## Conditions (machine rules)
- **caution**: `de > 1`

## Example
Jain Irrigation 3686.6/2175.5 = 1.69.
