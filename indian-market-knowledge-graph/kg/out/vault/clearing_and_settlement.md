# Clearing and Settlement (T+2 per source)

Buy on T day - funds + charges debited T; shares credited to demat end of T+2 (per source); sale proceeds credited T+2.

## Facts
- Contract note issued on T day for all trades.
- BTST (buy today sell tomorrow) sells shares not yet in demat - risk of short delivery, esp. in B-group/illiquid stocks.
- Sold shares are blocked immediately.

## Caveats
- Source describes T+2; India moved to T+1 settlement in 2023 - verify current cycle.
