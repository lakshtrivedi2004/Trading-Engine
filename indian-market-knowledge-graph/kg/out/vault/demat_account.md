# DEMAT Account

Electronic vault holding dematerialised shares, maintained by a depository (NSDL or CDSL) through a Depository Participant.

## Facts
- Paper certificates converted to electronic form post-1996 ('dematerialisation').
- Only two depositories in India - NSDL and CDSL.
- You open a DEMAT account through a DP (agent of the depository).
