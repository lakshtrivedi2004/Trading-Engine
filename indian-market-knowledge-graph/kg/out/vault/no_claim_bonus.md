# No Claim Bonus (NCB)

Cover grows (e.g. +50% of base) for each claim-free year up to a cap (50%-200% depending on plan); some plans claw it back after a claim, never below base. Verdict - not a gimmick.

## Example
Rs 10L base, 50% NCB -> 15L after 1 claim-free year, 20L after 2; a claim drops it back to 15L.
