# Base Metals (Copper, Aluminium, Lead, Nickel)

Copper - 3rd most consumed metal, global-growth sensitive (China+Japan ~half of demand); aluminium - abundant, power-intensive (17.4 MWh/t), tracks LME; lead - stable supply-demand, range-bound, batteries; nickel - ~65% to stainless steel, oversupplied. Trade price action, not fundamentals, for short horizons.
