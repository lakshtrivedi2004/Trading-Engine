# Equity (Shares)

Part-ownership of a publicly listed company traded on BSE/NSE; highest long-run return, no capital guarantee.

## Facts
- A share represents part ownership; owner entitled to dividends, bonus, splits, rights, voting.
- Buying a share means taking the same kind of risk as the promoter, scaled by holding.
