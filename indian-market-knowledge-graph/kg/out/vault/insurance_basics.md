# Insurance (Risk Transfer) - basics

Insurance transfers the financial risk of rare, high-cost events to an insurer in exchange for a premium; health and term life insurance come before an emergency fund and before investing. Insurers price risk through pooling and are exposed to adverse selection and moral hazard, which explains most policy restrictions.

## Facts
- Order of priority - health insurance + term insurance -> emergency corpus -> goal investing.
- Restrictions (waiting periods, co-pay, exclusions) exist to contain customer and hospital abuse.

## Bot use
Capital-protection precondition - a trading account should never be the emergency or medical fund.
