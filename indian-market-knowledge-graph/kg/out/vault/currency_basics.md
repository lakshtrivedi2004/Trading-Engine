# Currency Market Basics (Forex)

Currencies evolved from barter -> metal -> gold standard -> Bretton Woods (USD pegged to gold) -> floating market rates; forex trades ~$5.4T/day (2013 BIS), 24h x 6 days following the sun; participants include central banks, corporates (hedging), banks, travellers, traders.

## Facts
- Most active window when US
- UK
- Japan and Australia overlap; India's time zone overlaps Asia and Europe.
