# Taxation of Trading as Business Income

Speculative and non-speculative business income is added to other income and taxed at slab rates; expenses can be claimed.

## Facts
- Pros - low tax if total income low; claim expenses (brokerage, STT, charges, proportional internet/phone/rent, computer depreciation, staff salary, advisory, books); set off non-speculative losses against other non-salary income same year; carry forward.
- Cons - up to 30% slab on profits; ITR-4 (now ITR-3) needs CA; books and possible audit.

## Example
Salary 10L + F&O 1L + intraday 1L -> slab tax on 12L = 1.85L; plus STCG 1L @15% = 15k.
