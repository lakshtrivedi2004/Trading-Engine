# RBI Monetary Policy (Repo, Reverse Repo, CRR)

RBI balances growth vs inflation via key rates; reviewed periodically (quarterly per source).

## Facts
- Repo - rate at which RBI lends to banks; higher = costlier borrowing, slower growth; markets dislike hikes.
- Reverse repo - rate at which RBI borrows from banks; hike tightens money supply.
- CRR - share of deposits banks must keep with RBI; higher CRR drains liquidity.
- Rate-sensitive sectors react first - banks, autos, housing finance, real estate, metals.
- Higher rates curb inflation; lower rates tend to raise inflation.

Event-day volatility regime; sector rotation on rate decisions.
