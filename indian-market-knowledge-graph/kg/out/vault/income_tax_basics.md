# Income Tax Basics (FY, AY, slabs)

Tax on income under Income-tax Act 1961; FY = year income earned (Apr 1 - Mar 31); AY = following year in which it is filed; IT dept sees all PAN-linked market activity.

## Facts
- FY14-15 individual slabs (<60) - 0-2.5L nil; 2.5-5L 10%; 5-10L 25k + 20%; >10L 1.25L + 30%.
- Only ~2.9% of Indians paid tax (2015).

## Caveats
- Slabs and regimes have changed (new tax regime introduced 2020).
