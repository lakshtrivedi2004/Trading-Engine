# Price to Sales (P/S)

Price relative to sales per share; useful when earnings are cyclical or distorted.

## Formula
- **ps**: `price / (total_revenue / shares)`

## Caveats
- Higher-margin firms justify higher P/S - check profit margin.

## Example
ARBL 661/203.86 = 3.24x.
