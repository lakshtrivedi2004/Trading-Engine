# Investing

Deploying surplus cash into asset classes to fight inflation, create wealth and meet financial goals.

## Facts
- {"Idle cash loses to inflation; example": "Rs 20k/month surplus held as cash for 20 yrs ~ Rs 1.7 Cr vs ~Rs 4.26 Cr invested at 12%."}
- Corpus is highly sensitive to rate of return; small rate changes have large compounding impact.
- Reasons to invest - fight inflation, create wealth, meet life's financial aspirations.
